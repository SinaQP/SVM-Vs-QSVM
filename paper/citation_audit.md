## Statement Typology
To ensure methodological clarity, all manuscript assertions are audited under three explicit categories:
* **Category A (Prior Literature):** Theoretical, mathematical, or empirical claims established by earlier publications. Requires specific scholarly citation.
* **Category B (Method Definition):** Operational definitions, circuit formulations, or algorithmic workflows used in this study, citing foundational frameworks.
* **Category C (This Study's Empirical Result):** Primary empirical findings measured directly from our frozen `v1.0.0` canonical results. Must cite repository artifacts, NOT external literature.
